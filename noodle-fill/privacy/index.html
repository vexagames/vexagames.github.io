Noodle Fill: One Line Puzzle
Privacy Policy

Effective date: 6 October 2026 · Developer: Vexa

This policy explains what information is handled when you play Noodle Fill: One Line Puzzle (the "game") on Android, published by Vexa ("we", "us"). The short version: we do not ask for any personal information, the game has no accounts, and your progress stays on your device. The game shows optional ads through Google AdMob, and Google collects some device information to deliver them.

1. Information we collect ourselves

None. The game has no sign-up, no login, no chat and no online profile. We do not run our own servers or analytics for the game, and we do not receive your name, email address, contacts, photos, files or precise location.

2. Information stored on your device

Your game progress is saved locally on your device: coins, current level, stars, hints, unlocked and selected themes, daily streak and the sound setting. This data is not sent to us. If Android backup is switched on for your device, this progress may be included in the device backup that Google manages for your Google account.

3. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game contains rewarded video ads. An ad is only shown when you choose to watch one in exchange for an in-game reward. Ads are provided by Google AdMob. To deliver ads, limit how often you see the same ad, prevent fraud and measure performance, Google's advertising software in the game may collect and process:

  • device identifiers, including the Android advertising ID;
  • IP address, which can indicate your approximate location (city or region);
  • information about how you interact with ads and with the game, such as ad views and taps;
  • diagnostic information, such as device model, operating system version, language and crash or performance data.

Google acts under its own privacy policy when processing this information. You can read how Google uses information from apps that use its services at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites and Google's privacy policy at policies.google.com/privacy.

4. Your choices

  • Do not watch ads. Every ad reward in the game can also be obtained with in-game coins.
  • Reset or delete your advertising ID. On your Android device open Settings → Privacy → Ads (the exact wording varies by device) to reset or delete the advertising ID, which limits ad personalisation.
  • Consent in Europe. If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, you are asked for your advertising consent choices where the law requires it. Where this applies, you can change your choice at any time from the Privacy Policy screen inside the game.
  • Delete your game data. Uninstall the game, or open Settings → Apps → Noodle Fill → Storage → Clear storage. Because we hold no data about you, there is nothing for us to delete on our side.

5. Children

The game is intended for a general audience aged 13 and over. It is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the game, please contact us at the address below.

6. Sharing and selling of data

We do not sell personal information, and we do not share it with anyone, because we do not collect it. The only third party that receives information through the game is Google, as described in section 3.

7. Security and retention

Game progress stays on your device and is protected by the security of your device. It is kept until you clear the game's storage or uninstall the game. Information processed by Google for advertising is kept according to Google's own policies.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy, for example when features are added to the game. The current version is always available inside the game and at the address shown on the game's Google Play page. The effective date at the top shows when it was last changed.

9. Contact
