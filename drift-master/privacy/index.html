Drift Master
Privacy Policy

Effective date: 31 October 2026 · Developer: Vexa

This policy explains what information is handled when you play Drift Master (the "game") on Android, published by Vexa ("we", "us"). The short version: we do not ask for any personal information, the game has no accounts, and your progress stays on your device. The game shows optional ads through Google AdMob, and it uses Google Analytics for Firebase to tell us how the game is played. Google collects some device information to provide these services.

1. Information we ask you for

None. The game has no sign-up, no login, no chat and no online profile. We do not run our own servers for the game, and we do not receive your name, email address, contacts, photos, files or precise location. The only usage information we receive is the analytics described in section 4.

2. Information stored on your device

Your game progress is saved locally on your device: coins, cars and upgrades, completed missions and levels, daily rewards, best runs and your settings (including control layout). Your save itself is not sent to us; only the game events listed in section 4 are. If Android backup is switched on for your device, this progress may be included in the device backup that Google manages for your Google account.

3. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game contains rewarded video ads. An ad is only shown when you choose to watch one in exchange for an in-game reward, such as continuing a run, doubling your coins or trying a car. Ads are provided by Google AdMob. To deliver ads, limit how often you see the same ad, prevent fraud and measure performance, Google's advertising software in the game may collect and process:

  • device identifiers, including the Android advertising ID;
  • IP address, which can indicate your approximate location (city or region);
  • information about how you interact with ads and with the game, such as ad views and taps;
  • diagnostic information, such as device model, operating system version, language and crash or performance data.

Google acts under its own privacy policy when processing this information. You can read how Google uses information from apps that use its services at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites and Google's privacy policy at policies.google.com/privacy.

4. Game analytics (Google Analytics for Firebase)

The game uses Google Analytics for Firebase so we can see where players get stuck, how the game's difficulty and rewards are working, and which parts to improve. We see this data as reports about how the game is played, and it is not used to show you ads.

The game sends these events:

  • game sessions, and your total runs and coins when a session starts;
  • tutorial steps reached or skipped;
  • runs started and ended: the car, mission and driver level, how long the run lasted, how it ended, police cars wrecked and coins earned;
  • ads offered, shown and watched to the end, and where in the game;
  • cars and upgrades bought with in-game coins, and cars tried;
  • daily rewards, daily missions, league results and missions cleared;
  • whether you answered "rate" or "later" to the rating request.

Google Analytics for Firebase also automatically collects:

  • an app instance ID, a random identifier for this installation of the game, which changes if you reinstall the game or clear its storage;
  • IP address, used to work out your approximate location (country or region);
  • device and app information, such as device model, operating system version, language, app version, first open and time spent in the game.

We have switched off the collection of the Android advertising ID, ad storage, ad user data and ad personalisation in the game's analytics. This does not change what Google AdMob collects, as described in section 3.

You can read more at firebase.google.com/support/privacy.

5. Your choices

  • Do not watch ads. Ads are never forced. You can play the whole game without watching one.
  • Reset or delete your advertising ID. On your Android device open Settings → Privacy → Ads (the exact wording varies by device) to reset or delete the advertising ID, which limits ad personalisation.
  • Stop game analytics. The game has no separate switch for analytics, and your advertising consent choices do not change it. Analytics stops when you uninstall the game. Clearing the game's storage gives you a new app instance ID, and it also erases your progress.
  • Consent in Europe. If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, you are asked for your advertising consent choices where the law requires it. Where this applies, you can change your choice at any time from the game's Settings screen.
  • Delete your game data. Uninstall the game, or open Settings → Apps → Drift Master → Storage → Clear storage. Your progress is held only on your device.
  • Delete your analytics data or ask about it. The analytics data has no name or contact details in it, so we may not be able to tell which data is yours. Email us at the address below and we will help as far as we can. Depending on where you live, you may have legal rights to access or delete your personal data.

6. Children

The game is intended for a general audience aged 13 and over. It is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the game, please contact us at the address below.

7. Sharing and selling of data

We do not sell personal information. The only third party that receives information through the game is Google, as described in sections 3 and 4. Google may process this information on servers outside your country.

8. Security and retention

Game progress stays on your device and is protected by the security of your device. It is kept until you clear the game's storage or uninstall the game. Information processed by Google for advertising is kept according to Google's own policies. In Google Analytics, event data linked to an individual installation is automatically deleted after the retention period set for the game, at most 14 months. Summary reports with totals may be kept for longer.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy, for example when features are added to the game. The current version is always available at the address shown on the game's Google Play page. The effective date at the top shows when it was last changed.
